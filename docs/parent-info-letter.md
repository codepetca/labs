# Codepet Labs Parent Info Letter

Subject: Codepet Labs invitation

Hello parents,

Your student has been invited to join Codepet Labs this summer. I know them
from my Grade 11 Computer Science course this past semester, where they were
one of the top performers, and I thought they could be a strong fit for this
independent summer builder lab.

Codepet Labs is an independent, startup-style student builder lab operated by
Codepet.ca. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or operated by any school.

Labs works more like a small startup than a class: students use AI tools,
GitHub, and regular feedback to build, test, demo, and improve small software
projects.

Students will use standard online tools for communicating, writing, saving, and
publishing software projects. They will also need access to a paid AI coding
assistant at a low-tier plan. Some tools offer free plans, but the lab expects
students to use a paid plan so they can work regularly with AI support.
Students are responsible for deciding which tool they use.

The 8-week summer format is:

- weekly 2 hour online video check-ins for collaborative review
- about 10 hours per week of independent learning and building
- final demo and wrap-up

You can read more here: [codepet labs](https://labs.codepet.ca/about)

Feel free to email me with any questions or concerns.

Warm regards,
Stewart Chan
