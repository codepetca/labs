# CodePet Labs Parent Info Letter

Subject: CodePet Labs invitation

Hello parents,

Your student was in my Grade 11 Computer Science course this past semester. They
have been invited to join CodePet Labs this summer.

CodePet Labs is an independent, startup-style student builder lab operated by
Codepet.ca. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or operated by any school.

Labs works more like a small startup than a class: students use AI tools,
GitHub, and regular feedback to build, test, demo, and improve small software
projects.

The 8-week summer format is:

- weekly 1 hr online video check-in and direction setting
- about 10 hours per week of independent learning and building
- final demo and wrap-up

Students will use personal accounts with third-party developer tools such as
GitHub, ChatGPT, and Discord. These accounts are not created or managed by
CodePet Labs. Each tool has its own terms, age requirements, privacy policy, and
parental-consent rules. Families are responsible for reviewing those terms and
confirming which tools their student may use.

Students and families should choose AI tools that fit the student's project
needs, eligibility, and budget. Some tools offer limited free tiers, while
others offer optional paid plans. CodePet Labs does not require families to
purchase a specific subscription.

Students will not be required to use a tool they are not eligible to use.

You can read more here: [CodePet Labs About link]

Feel free to email me with any questions or concerns.

Warm regards,

CodePet Labs
